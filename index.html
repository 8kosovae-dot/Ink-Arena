HOW IT WORKS

About Ink Arena

A straightforward guide to matches, scoring, rankings, and the community.

01 / PLAYING A MATCH

Choose a mode and time

Use public matchmaking for a quick match, or host a private room and share its 8-character code. Private rooms hold up to 30 writers, including guests. The host can start once at least 2 people have joined, or the room starts automatically when all 30 seats fill. Everyone receives the same prompt.

  • Lightning · 1 minute: up to 500 characters
  • 5 minutes: up to 2,500 characters
  • 10 minutes: up to 5,000 characters
  • 15 minutes: up to 7,500 characters
  • 20 minutes: up to 10,000 characters
  • 30 minutes: up to 10,000 characters

The timer begins when the host starts a private room or public matchmaking fills. At zero, each writer's latest autosaved text is submitted automatically.

02 / PROMPT SKIP

Change a prompt together

Every player can vote to skip the current prompt. The prompt changes only when everyone votes yes. A random new prompt is chosen and the full timer restarts. Voting closes after anyone submits.

03 / AI GRADING · 120 POINTS

Six criteria, 20 points each

Craft & clarity 20 pts

Clear, effective sentences, vivid details, and a consistent writing voice.

Story & structure 20 pts

Interesting development, coherent events, characters or ideas, and a satisfying shape.

Prompt fit 20 pts

How well the story responds to the shared prompt and uses its central idea.

Emotion & impact 20 pts

How effectively the story creates a mood, feeling, tension, humour, or memorable effect.

Originality 20 pts

Fresh ideas, distinctive choices, and an approach that feels specific rather than predictable.

Spelling & grammar 20 pts

Spelling, punctuation, grammar, and sentence correctness. Intentional voice and dialect are not automatically mistakes.

All six scores are added for a total out of 120. The highest total wins; matching top scores mean a tie. The AI score is feedback, not an unquestionable verdict.

04 / ELO & GUESTS

Ranked or unranked

Regular accounts start with an ELO rating. In ranked matches, ratings and win/loss/draw records update after judging. Guest matches are always unranked. If even one guest joins a match, nobody in that match gains or loses ELO or record points.

05 / SAVING & SHARING

Your stories, your choice

After a match has been judged, a registered account can save its own story privately to the Profile page or publish it to the public Gallery. Publishing is optional. Signed-in, non-guest accounts can give other writers a thumbs-up. Stories with the most thumbs-up appear first.

06 / ACCOUNTS

Choose how to sign in

Use email and password for a permanent profile, saved stories, and ELO, or continue temporarily as a guest. Guests can join private rooms with a code, but cannot host. Guest sessions cannot be recovered after sign-out and never affect ELO.

07 / COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Make this a good place to write

Ink Arena is a shared writing space. Keep these simple rules in mind when competing, commenting, and publishing.

  • Respect other writers. Critique the writing, not the person. No harassment, insults, threats, or targeting another player.
  • Keep stories suitable for a teen-accessible community. Avoid graphic, sexual, or otherwise inappropriate material.
  • Protect personal information. Do not include real full names, addresses, phone numbers, school details, private messages, or identifying information.
  • Share your own work. Do not pass off someone else's writing as yours or try to manipulate scores and likes.
  • Think before publishing. Public Gallery stories can be read by anyone. Only publish writing you are comfortable sharing publicly.
  • Use the platform fairly. Do not spam likes, impersonate other writers, or disrupt matches.

Stories are visible to match participants and are processed by automated screening and AI judging. Automated systems can make mistakes; report issues through the available site controls.
